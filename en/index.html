One turn, three beats

  1. Your energy grows and you draw a card.
  2. You play cards and your hero's abilities.
  3. You close with one action: attack, heavy strike or pass.

Fair by design

  • Power is never for sale: everything can be earned by playing.
  • No card trading between players.
  • No ads.
  • Pack odds shown in plain sight in the game.
