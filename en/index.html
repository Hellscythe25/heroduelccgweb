Hero Duel CCG

A hero-versus-hero duel with no creature board: instants, equipment and energy instead of mana. Your hero, your deck, one duel.

Discover the gameLearn to play

  • No creature board

    All damage goes straight to the enemy hero. Duels you can read at a glance.

  • Weapon, armor and trinket

    Three equipment slots that wear down, break and get replaced.

  • Energy instead of mana

    It grows by 1 each turn up to 10 and fully refills. Every point counts.

Choose your hero

Every hero has their own life, passive and abilities. You start with one and unlock the rest by playing their campaign.

One turn, three beats

  1. Your energy grows and you draw a card.
  2. You play cards and your hero's abilities.
  3. You close with one action: attack, heavy strike or pass.

Fair by design

  • Power is never for sale: everything can be earned by playing.
  • No card trading between players.
  • No ads.
  • Pack odds shown in plain sight in the game.
