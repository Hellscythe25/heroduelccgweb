The game

One duel, two heroes

Hero Duel CCG is a competitive, one-on-one, turn-based collectible card game. Every duel pits two heroes against each other, and all damage goes straight to the enemy hero.

What sets it apart

  • No creature board

    No armies to summon: damage goes straight to the enemy hero. Fewer pieces on the table and duels you can read at a glance.

  • Three equipment pillars

    A weapon to strike, armor to endure and a trinket to surprise. One slot for each.

  • Energy that grows

    You start with no energy and your maximum goes up by 1 every turn, up to 10. It fully refills on each of your turns.

  • Short matches, dense decisions

    Each hero's abilities turn spare energy into blows that close out the match instead of dragging it on.

  • Fair matches

    The server decides every rule, every point of damage and every reward. No one can peek at your hand or give themselves an edge from their own machine.

Game modes

Against other players

Ranked
Climb the ranks with every win. Matchmaking looks for opponents close to your level.
Casual
Unrated matches in a queue of their own that never mixes with ranked.
Private room
Share a 6-character code and challenge anyone you like.

Campaigns against the AI

Every hero has their own campaign. Its duels play out on your machine with no turn timer, and the server only hands out the rewards. Clearing a hero's campaign unlocks that hero along with their starter deck.

How you start

When you create your profile you pick a starter hero and receive their 20-card deck. Every other hero is earned by playing their campaign. Game modes are never locked.

Cards and collection

There are neutral cards, which any hero can use, and cards exclusive to each hero. A deck has 20 cards.

  • Instant

    A one-shot effect that resolves right away.

  • Weapon

    Adds its power to your attacks until its durability runs out.

  • Armor

    Reduces the damage you take until its durability runs out.

  • Trinket

    Triggers on its own when its condition is met, spending a charge each time.

Rarities

Maximum copies of the same card in a deck:

  • Common×3
  • Uncommon×3
  • Rare×2
  • Epic×2
  • Legendary×1

Fair by design

  • Power is never for sale

    Everything you can buy is collection or style, and everything can be earned by playing.

  • No trading between players

    Cards are never traded or sold between players. Your collection is yours alone.

  • No ads

    No rewarded ads and no full-screen ads.

  • Odds in plain sight

    Pack odds and guarantees are shown in the game, and there is always a direct path to any card: crafting it with essence.

Platforms and languages

PC (Windows) first. The game is available in Spanish and English.
