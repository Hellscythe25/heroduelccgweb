2. What data we process

2.1 Your game account

  • Player identifier. The first time you open the game, Unity’s authentication service creates an anonymous account with a random identifier. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, address or date of birth.
  • Display name. The name you choose in the game (3 to 16 characters). Other players will see it (see section 3).
  • Unity account (when available). If you later link your progress to a Unity account, Unity handles your credentials; the game never receives your password. [PENDING: confirm which Unity account data reaches the game before this feature is turned on.]

2.2 Your progress and activity in the game

The game’s servers store:

  • Profile: display name, creation date, ranked score, win streak, starter hero and unlocked heroes, and the daily coin counter.
  • Card collection, decks (including the names you give them) and unopened packs.
  • Balances of the game’s virtual currencies (coins, essence and gems) and the pack guarantee counters.
  • History of your last 20 matches: heroes, result, turns, how the match ended, score change and the opponent’s display name.
  • Campaign progress.
  • Purchase record: transaction identifiers and one-time products.
  • Data for each online match: the match state (both players’ hands, decks, equipment and random seed), the result and the reward settlement, and your entry in the matchmaking queue.

2.3 Technical and usage data (analytics)

Only with your consent, the game sends usage events to Unity Analytics, such as a match starting and ending, ability use, campaign progress, pack opening, card crafting, purchases and saved decks. Unity’s analytics service adds device data: model, operating system, processor and graphics card, memory, screen, language, game version, platform and its own installation identifiers.

[PENDING: today the game does not ask for this consent, and analytics is switched off in the Unity dashboard. Before this policy is published, the game must ask for consent on first launch and let players withdraw it in Options.]

[PENDING: confirm in Unity’s documentation whether its analytics service processes the IP address or an approximate location.]

2.4 Server logs

The game’s servers keep technical logs to run the service, find bugs and prevent cheating. They may include your player identifier, match and transaction identifiers, and your score.

2.5 Data stored on your device

The game stores your preferences on your PC (language, audio, video, effects and controller vibration), your Unity sign-in session, a copy of the card catalog and a technical log file (Player.log) that includes your player identifier. This data stays on your device, except the session, which is used to sign you in.

2.6 What we do not process

  • There are no ads and no advertising tracking.
  • There is no chat: the only communication with other players is the match itself.
  • During the beta there are no real-money payments, so we do not process payment data (see section 9).

2.7 This website

This site uses no cookies, has no analytics and has no forms. Like any web server, our hosting provider’s server logs technical data about each visit (IP address, browser, requested page and time) for security and operation. [PENDING: how long the provider keeps those logs.]

3. What other players see

  • Your opponent in a match sees your display name and your hero, and your name stays in their match history.
  • If you play ranked matches, the leaderboard shows any player the top 20 positions with their display name and score.

That is why we ask that your display name does not include your real name or other personal data.
