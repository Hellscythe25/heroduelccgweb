Hero Duel CCG's first closed beta has begun. It's the first time the game runs
on our own server, and 22 testers are already playing by invitation.

## Why our own server

For digital sovereignty and autonomy: we want to decide for ourselves where
the game lives and how it works.

## How to get in

The first time you open the game, you type your invite code. After that, it
won't ask again on that device.

At the start you choose your hero and your name. You can play as a guest or
create your account so you don't lose your progress if you switch devices.

## What you can play

- **Six heroes**, each with their own campaign against the AI and their own
  pack.
- **PVP** in ranked, casual or a private room to challenge your friends.
- **Season 0**, which lasts the whole beta, with its reward pass and a medal
  when it closes.

What each version of the game brings is in the [Changelog](/en/changelog/).

## Thank you

To the 22 testers already playing: thank you for trying the game before anyone
else.
