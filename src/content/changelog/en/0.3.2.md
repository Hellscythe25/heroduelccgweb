### New

- **Full collection**: the Collection now shows ALL the cards in the game — the ones you don't have yet appear in gray, so you know exactly what you're missing from each set.
- **Type filter in Collection**: besides hero and set, you can now filter by Instant, Weapon, Armor or Trinket.
- **Win streak (ranked)**: winning in a row pays more — 1st and 2nd win +10 points, 3rd +13, 4th +16, and from the 5th on +20 (the cap for now). A ranked loss resets the streak; casual matches don't affect it. Your active streak shows next to your rank in the menu.
- **New cards in Penumbral Shadows**: the set's legendary weapon and armor arrive — **Duskblade** (weapon: +5 power, 4 durability) and **Aegis of Eternal Night** (armor: reduces 5 per hit, 4 durability), both costing 7.

### Card balance

- **Apocalypse** (neutral, legendary): damage 20 → **5**. Winning the match in a single blow was not the plan.
