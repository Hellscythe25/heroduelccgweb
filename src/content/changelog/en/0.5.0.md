### New

- **Combat effects**: hits now land with impact. When you take damage you see an impact flash, the health number and a trail on the bar, and armor flashes too. Gear settles in when equipped, wears down, breaks and gets repaired with its own effect. Turn, energy, max health, deck, the opponent's hand and their coin pulse when they change. When the match ends, victory, defeat and the no-combat ending each get their own moment.
- **Your played cards carry your hero's signature**: embers for Brakka, a steel ring for Sable, threads for Yrenna, shadow and moon for Shouny and dust for Krunk.
- **More exciting packs**: each card is revealed according to its rarity, with a jump and a summary at the end. Rare, Epic and Legendary cards also shine when you inspect them.
- **The Divine Pack gets its own ceremony**: if you get a pack where all five cards are Epic or Legendary, it is announced with a two-tone light and the cards are revealed one after another, each with more force and with pauses to protect your eyes.
- **More things you notice while playing**: when you draw, cards come from your deck (for the opponent you only see card backs); you see discards fall; one-turn bonus energy appears as a separate crystal; and the Challenger's Obol spins and crumbles into golden dust when used, without going to the discard pile.
- **Crafting and rewards with effects**: when you craft, essence flows into the card; when you convert duplicates, they dissolve into essence; first-win rewards for each level fly into your wallet and your packs; and when you choose the boss exclusive, the others fade out. Replays that don't pay show nothing.
- **You choose the effects**: in Options you can set the effect speed (Normal, Fast or No animation) and turn on “Reduce motion and flashes.” You can also choose the damage number color: Neutral, the default, or Red.
- **No more repeated names**: Brakka's card “War Cry” is renamed **Iron Bellow**, and Sable's heal “Living Wall” becomes **Second Wind**. Their effects don't change. Second Wind gets new art: Sable kneeling behind her shield, catching her breath.
- **All three heroes get new abilities!** All six ability slots were reworked so each hero plays differently:
  - **Brakka Iron Fury**: **Unleashed Fury** (2 energy, +1 damage that stacks as much as you like) and **Devastating Blow** (6 energy, +5 to your strike — the most efficient finisher in the game).
  - **Sable the Guardian**: **Rivet** (1 energy, repairs 1 point of her armor) and **Colossus Slam** (6 energy, a strike that hits harder the better the armor she has equipped).
  - **Yrenna the Arcane Weaver**: **Void Lance** (3 energy, 2 direct damage that goes through armor, once per turn) and **Reweave** (6 energy, recycles your whole hand and draws a new one).
- **2 new heroes arrive!** Joining the universe are **Shouny the Deadly Shadow** (24 health: empties your hand and finishes you off when you have nothing left) and **Krunk Fist of Demolition** (30 health: breaks the opponent's weapons, armor and trinkets, and draws a card each time he does), each with their own starter deck and exclusive cards.
- **Gear repair now has a cap**: repairing can no longer raise durability above the original (plus any upgrades that increase it). No more infinite armor.
- **Preparing the future of trinkets**: trinkets will soon be able to trigger on many more conditions — the groundwork is in place, and the new cards that use it will arrive with upcoming sets.
- **The game starts in your language**: the first time you open it, it uses your system language if there is a translation (currently Spanish and English); otherwise, Spanish. If you already chose a language in the options, that one is kept.
- **The second player in PvP gets compensation.** Going first is an advantage, so whoever goes second starts with an extra card, **Challenger's Obol** (cost 0: you gain 1 energy for that turn only; your opponent can see you have it until you play it) and with **+2 health**, both current and max. It applies to quick, ranked and code matches. Campaigns don't change.
- **More art on your cards!** 101 cards that used to show a placeholder now have their own art, and **Krunk** and **Shouny** get portraits. The hero selection tiles now show each hero's face, with the name below.
- **Play with a keyboard or controller**: you can navigate the whole game (lobby, campaigns, collection, decks, shop, packs and combat) with the arrow keys or the D-pad. The active element is highlighted, Accept selects and Cancel goes back. In combat, focus starts on your first playable card, and focusing a card opens its large view. Help texts also appear on focus. In the lobby, the D-pad moves down from the PVP and PVE tiles to Shop, Collection, Packs, Decks and Options; with Options open, navigation stays inside the panel; and long lists scroll on their own to show what you focus. With the mouse, everything stays the same.
- **Easier to read**:
  - No text is smaller than 12 pixels.
  - Your balances show their currency icon: gold or essence.
  - Scroll bars (collection, packs, history…) use the game's colors instead of showing up white.
  - On mobile, the game always stays in landscape and respects the screen notch.
- **Combat with scenery**: every match is played in the opponent hero's arena:
  - Brakka's volcanic forge;
  - Sable's wall;
  - Yrenna's arcane loom;
  - Shouny's moonlit rooftops;
  - Krunk's quarry.

  Against other opponents, you play in the forge hall. A dark veil keeps everything readable.
- **Clearer shop**:
  - each pack shows its hero's portrait;
  - drop rates open in a separate window, with their own percentage button, without moving the shop.
- **Collection**: your essence balance is shown with its icon, just like in the lobby and the shop.
- **See every hit**: when a hero loses or recovers health, a floating number (“-3” in red, “+2” in green) appears right where their bar went down or up, and fades in an instant. It never blocks your actions or covers health, energy or your hand.
- **Your hero in the lobby**: the circle next to your name shows the portrait of the hero you chose.
- **Engine updated**: the game moves to a new version of its engine, with internal stability and performance improvements. It looks the same, except for details like the victory message, which now fits on a single line.
- **The Challenger's Obol gets a frame**: the extra card for whoever goes second in PvP has a neutral gold frame, since it has no rarity, instead of the gray of common cards.
- **Clean combat log when replaying a stage**: it used to carry over data from the previous match (for example, “the enemy recovers 20 health” at the start).
- **The whole game in one style**: the 18 cards that already had art were repainted in the new style, along with 15 others that didn't fit. **Brakka**, **Sable** and **Yrenna** get new portraits, and the campaign covers and the lobby's PVP and PVE entries have new art.

### Campaign changes

- **Each hero now has their own campaign, made of 4 fights against that same hero**: you face them on **Easy, Normal, Hard and Boss**, in that order — each win opens the next fight, and the opponent is tougher and plays better each time.
  - **Easy** and **Normal**: they are the path. They pay no reward: they open the next fight.
  - **Hard**: **you unlock the hero and their starter deck**, and from then on you can buy and open their packs.
  - **Boss**: **10 packs of that hero** + **you choose 1 of their exclusive cards** from the ones they keep only for whoever defeats them. The rest of their cards drop from packs, as always.
- **The classic 10-level campaign stays as it is**, visible and with no locks, **paying 460 coins and 115 essence in total**. It's where you make gold — and you spend that gold on whichever pack you want.
- **Mini packs can no longer be bought.** They become a gift: you'll get them from events, codes and compensation.
- **The campaign AI plays fair.** Before, when calculating its moves, the AI could “know” which cards you had in hand and how randomness would turn out (draws, random discards…), and that could look like luck. Now it decides only with what a player in its place would see: it imagines several possibilities for what it can't see and picks the move that works best across all of them. The difficulty of each fight doesn't change.

### Pack changes

- **Packs are now PER HERO**: each hero has their own pack, with their own cards and their own legendaries — you know exactly where what you're looking for drops. To buy a hero's packs, you first have to clear **their campaign on Hard difficulty** (except your starter hero's, which you can buy from the first minute). The shop reorganizes its catalog by hero — look for the one you want to equip and you'll find all their packs.
- **The GOD PACK arrives!** One in every 500 packs comes out blessed: **all its cards are epic or legendary**. The Drop rates screen shows this exact figure (1 in 500) along with all the other chances of your pack.
- **Apocalypse is removed from packs** and becomes the game's **first gift card**: it no longer drops from any pack (it can still be crafted) and its special version will be given away at events. It also now deals **2 damage** (was 5) — it was still too much for a card that is free to play.

### New content

- **Expansion 0 joins the catalog**: **39 new cards** for Shouny, Krunk and neutrals — of every rarity, including the exclusive legendaries each hero gives up only when you defeat their boss. Highlights: **The War Drum** and **The Titan's Blow**, two new legendaries for Brakka that were missing from his set. You'll find them in the per-hero packs.
- **Pack names**: each pack has its own identity — **The Iron Roar** (Brakka), **The Wall's Temper** (Sable), **The Weave of the Void** (Yrenna), **The Threads of Penumbra** (Shouny) and **The Forge's Spoils** (Krunk). The Collection can now be browsed by filtering **by pack**, as well as by expansion — it's easier to see what each one brings and build a specialized deck.

### Economy

- **You now choose a hero when you start.** When you create your profile you pick one of the heroes and start with their starter deck and 200 coins. **You earn the other heroes by playing THEIR campaign**: when you finish it on Hard difficulty you get the hero and their full deck, ready to play. No game mode is ever locked — from the first minute you can play ranked and casual with your starter hero.
- **Daily gold cap: 250 per day.** Quick and ranked matches give **50 coins per win and 20 per loss** (was 100 and 40), and they add to the same daily counter, which resets every day at 00:00 UTC. With this, a session of 5 to 8 matches pays out in full instead of stopping paying at the third. **The cap only affects gold**: your ranking points and your streak keep going up even if you reach it. Campaigns and challenges pay separately, without counting toward the cap.

### Visual improvements

- **Inspect any card up close**: hover over it (on PC) or press and hold the card (on touch) to see it full screen with all its art.
- **Your hand shows each card's art**: the cards in your hand show their art in a clearly visible strip.
- **Campaigns with art**: the campaign menu is now an illustrated grid, with the heroes' portraits and tiles of the same size.
- **New icons** in the main menu and in the gear slots.
- **Health is no longer shown as negative**: when a hero falls, their health stays at 0.
- Available art is shown in your hand, in the collection and when opening packs; cards that don't have art yet keep their previous look. The full text can be read in its tooltip when you hover. The cost and the play button stay visible when the hand squeezes the cards together.
- **Daily gold pill in the hub**: in the PVP menu, you see your daily coin progress live — “Today's gold: **N**/250” shows how much is left to reach the cap (it updates after every win or loss).
- **Hero unlocked banner in campaigns**: when you finish a hero's campaign on Hard difficulty and unlock them, a celebration screen shows you the new hero and their deck — ready to take into combat.
- **Boss exclusive card choice panel**: after defeating the boss, you don't get a random one — you choose the exclusive card you take (with a two-step confirmation to avoid accidents).
- **The campaign looks like a ladder**: the hero's 4 fights, one below the other, with what each one pays in view, the next one lit up and the cleared ones dimmed. You no longer choose difficulty in a separate menu: you choose it by entering the fight that's next.
- **Full-screen collection and packs**: the collection, pack opening and the boss exclusive choice show cards with full-bleed art. The ones you don't have yet look dimmed, and you can also inspect them up close.
- **Readable card names in your hand**: the name now takes up to two lines and adjusts on narrow cards. The Attack, abilities and Pass buttons fit on narrow screens, and at the end of the match the reward, rank and streak are shown on a more discreet line.

### Fixes

- **Full damage number**: if a hit deals more damage than the hero has left, the number shows the real damage and health stays at 0 (never negative).
- **The game no longer crashes when closing.**
- **More stable card inspection**: text no longer shrinks too much, clicks are no longer lost after pressing and holding a card, and the view shows up again when redrawn.
- **Campaign stages**: you can no longer start the same stage twice with a double click.
- **Simultaneous double death**: if a single hit leaves both heroes at 0 health, whoever played that action loses (when ending the turn, whoever ended it). Before, Player 1 always lost.
- **Card texts fixed**: Shouny's passive now says you gain the energy when the opponent is forced to discard cards **on your turn** (that's how it always worked), and **Night Veil** and **Scrap Plating** say “reduces 2 damage per hit, 3 durability” instead of “blocks 3 hits.”
- **Returning to the lobby and canceling the search**: returning to the lobby no longer leaves screens stuck or matches hanging, even if the update fails (the error is shown in the lobby), and canceling the search no longer abandons a match that had already found you an opponent: you join it.
- **Simultaneous match end**: if two actions ended the match at the same instant (for example, a lethal hit and a forfeit at the same time), the server could consider it over twice and hand out **gold, rank and history twice**. Now each match has **a single result** and is settled **only once**: the action that arrives late is rejected.
- **Interrupted match close**: if something failed right while rewards were being handed out, the match could be left **half done** (for example, with rank updated but no gold or history) and with no way to complete it. Now each match keeps a record of what has already been done, and when it is checked again **only what was missing is completed**, without repeating what was done. In the few cases where it can't be known whether a step was applied, the game prefers not to repeat it rather than pay you twice.
- **Purchases and openings at the same time**: buying packs with several clicks in a row, or opening packs while another purchase finishes, can no longer **make you lose a paid pack**, **make cards disappear** from your collection or **overwrite your guarantee (pity) counter**. Each purchase is charged first and added to whatever you have at that moment.
- **The last copy of a card**: if two purchases or crafts competed for the last copy you can own of a card, both could be charged. Now only one goes through, and the other operation **refunds you** what was charged. If your collection is busy with another operation and the card can't be added, **your payment is refunded too** and you can try again.
- **Rewards from a match left half done**: if a match ended but its close didn't complete and your opponent didn't open the game again, your rewards could go unpaid. Now, when you open the game, **what was missing is completed** (only once, without duplicating anything) before the pending match is cleared.
- **Repeated room codes**: when creating a room or searching for a quick match, the new code can no longer **overwrite another player's match** if it happens to match an existing one: another one is chosen.
- **Loading screen in your language**: the first time you open the game, the loading screen now comes up in the same language as the menu (your system's if there is a translation). Before, it could come up in Spanish even if the menu was in English.
- **Open packs without losing them**: if something fails while your packs are being opened, they go back to your inventory; before, they could disappear without giving you cards.
- **Convert duplicates without losing cards**: if the essence credit fails, your cards no longer disappear.
- **Decks kept safe**: saving or deleting decks in quick succession no longer loses a deck or goes over the limit of 3 per hero.
- **More reliable matches**: the turn timer no longer freezes at 0 s, and the screen no longer shows an old state of the match (wrong turn or a finished match that “comes back to life”). Also, no one outside a match can look it up, and room codes are harder to guess.
- **Trinkets that don't waste charges**: a trinket no longer spends a charge when its effect would be useless (for example, healing at full health, repairing intact armor or a +5 attack on the opponent's turn). Now every charge is an effect you can use.
- **Campaign**: if a level's reward couldn't be claimed, it now tells you (replay the level to claim it).
- **Heroes**: hero lists show only the ones you have unlocked.
- **Leaderboard**: the name shown is the one the player chose in the game, validated by the server.
- **Small fixes**: tooltips no longer get stuck; “Finding an opponent…” no longer shows up instead of your room code; texts fixed (“Atacas,” “Poco común,” “monedas” everywhere, a unified verb for healing and several English phrases) and new notices translated.
- **Deck names in your language**: starter decks and unnamed decks are shown in the language you chose.

### Important notice: your progress was reset

When updating to 0.5.0, **the game reset your progress**: collection, decks, coins, essence, unopened packs, campaign progress, rank, streak and history went back to the starting point. **You kept your player name.**

Why? Campaigns, packs and the economy changed from the ground up: heroes are now earned campaign by campaign and packs are per hero. Carrying over progress from the alpha would have left accounts halfway between two systems — for example, with old-model packs that no longer belong to any hero. Starting even was the clean way to launch the new structure.

**What you see when you log in**: the game asks you to **choose your starter hero**. You get their deck and 200 coins, and from there you earn the others by playing their campaigns. No mode is locked: you can play ranked and casual from the first minute.
