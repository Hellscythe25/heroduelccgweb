### New

- **Your account, on any computer:** in Options → Account you can link your game to an account and recover it on another PC. If you already had another game on that computer, you see both and choose which one to keep.

### Changes

- **Campaign:** stages are played in order. If you play offline, you can play, but the match gives no reward.
- **PvP matches:** matches that end before turn 4, and from the fourth of the day against the same opponent onward, give no reward. The reason appears when the match ends and in History.
