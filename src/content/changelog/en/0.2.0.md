First version handed out to testers. It included: 1v1 duels with heroes, abilities and equipment; quick (casual) and ranked matches with 14 ranks and a Leaderboard; collection, packs per set (Core and Penumbral Shadows), a deck builder with card preview on mouse hover; converting duplicate cards into essence; match history; options with language switching (Spanish/English); reconnecting to ongoing matches and a turn timer.
