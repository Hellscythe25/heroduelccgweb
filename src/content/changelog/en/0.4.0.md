### New

- **Revamped main menu**: you now pick a mode with two big buttons — **PVP** (quick matches, ranked and private rooms, with your History and the Leaderboard right there) and **PVE** (the Campaign). The Shop, Collection, Packs and Decks are still in the menu, and Options open as a side panel (with the quit game button inside).
- **The Campaign arrives!** A new single-player mode against the computer, **with no connection involved and no turn timer** — at your own pace. The PVE button opens the **campaign picker**: each campaign is a big tile with your progress (and a "Complete!" when you finish it), and when you enter it becomes **its own screen**, with the campaign's name and your progress in the title. You choose your hero and deck **right there**, with the campaign already open. Levels unlock in order; beating a level **for the first time** pays coins, essence and even cards; you can replay levels whenever you want, but only the first win gives a reward. When you win or lose a match **you go straight back to the campaign** — not to the menu — to move on to the next level. And the campaign **opens instantly**, without waiting on anything.
- **The Shop opens!** **Packs you can buy with coins**, in batches of 1 to 40 in a single tap. Purchased packs pile up **unopened** and you open them whenever you want from the Packs screen. And the **drop rates** screen shows the exact odds of each pack and its guarantees.
- **Card crafting**: essence finally has a use — in the Collection, tap a card you're missing and craft it instantly (Common 20 · Uncommon 40 · Rare 80 · Epic 160 · Legendary 400 essence). Double-click to confirm.

### Important notice

- **Progress reset for the beta**: the beta phase begins and alpha progress is reset for everyone — collection, decks, coins, essence, rank and history go back to their initial state (you keep your name). Thanks for testing the alpha! The balances and cards from the testing period were not representative of the game's real economy.

### Fixes

- **Duplicate matches when reconnecting**: closing the game in the middle of a match and coming back in let you search for ANOTHER match at the same time, creating "clones" of the same player and ghost opponents that never acted (sometimes with no name). Now the server won't let you join a second match: if you have one in progress and tap "search", the game takes you straight to your active match. Old waiting rooms that were left abandoned are also cleaned up automatically.
- **Menu and combat overlapping**: if you opened History (or another menu screen) while searching for a match and an opponent appeared right then, both interfaces ended up on top of each other. Now everything else closes when the match starts.
- **Packs: cut-off rewards**: when opening 6 or more packs, the cards you got went off screen with no way to see them. Now the card area scrolls.
- **Language not applied on restart**: if you played in English and closed the game, when you came back the loading screen was in English but the menus stayed in Spanish (with mixed texts). Now the saved language is fully applied from startup.
- **Ghost "Resume ongoing match" button**: sometimes the menu offered to resume a match that no longer existed (for example, if you closed the game while searching for an opponent or waiting in a room). Now the game checks at startup that the match is really still alive, and **closing the game cancels your pending search or room** — abandoned rooms are closed completely, so nobody can join a match against an opponent who is no longer there.
- **Loading screen stuck waiting forever**: if the connection failed in the middle of loading, the game could hang on a step without moving on or warning you. Now loading **retries on its own** (you'll see "Retrying...") up to twice, and only if that doesn't work either does the Retry button appear.

### Improvements

- **New "Dark Forge" visual identity**: the whole game gets a new look — deep charcoal with molten gold details, new typefaces and redesigned screens. Combat now has **health bars**, a counter showing **how many cards your opponent has in hand**, a **play log** and a central attack button; playable cards **lift up to stand out** (keeping their rarity color) and the ones you can't afford say so ("No energy"); the Packs button in the menu shows how many unopened packs you have.
- **The game's first images**: the emblem now shows on the loading screen and in the menu, and the three heroes debut their portraits — you'll see them when picking a hero and during combat. The PVP and PVE buttons in the menu now also have their own illustrated scene: two warriors clashing for PVP, and the lone hero heading for the citadel for PVE. It's placeholder art that we'll keep improving during the beta, but the game is starting to have a face of its own.
- **Bad-luck protection in packs**: a legendary card is guaranteed within your first 10 packs of each set, and you'll never go more than 40 packs in a row without a legendary. (Packs opened before this version don't count toward the counter.)
- **Clearer Collection**: the filters now sit in three separate rows (hero · set · card type), and the screen shows your essence — what you spend there.
- **Deck builder**: new filter by card **cost** (0 to 5+), in addition to the type filter.
- **More convenient packs**: an **x10** button to open the maximum in one tap, and the screen shows how many unopened packs you have of each set.
- **Equipment with rarity color**: in combat, the border of each equipped piece (weapon, armor, trinket) shows its card's rarity color — just like in your hand.
- **Revamped loading screen**: centered title and the game version at the bottom.
- **The interface scales well on any monitor**: on 4K and QHD screens the interface now looks the right size, without distortion — a uniform, crisp zoom at any 16:9 resolution.
