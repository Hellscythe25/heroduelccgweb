### New

- **Season 0:** a new button in the lobby header opens the Season, which lasts the whole beta. In "Pass" you see your level, the free and premium reward tracks with what you've already earned, what's next and what's left, and you can equip avatars and frames from there. "Archive" keeps your past seasons, also reachable from PVP History. Everything works with a controller.
- **Create your account at the start:** on the beta server, the first time you sign in you can choose "Create account": you type your player name and the account is created in the browser, linked to your game, so you don't lose your progress if you switch devices. If you already have one, "I already have an account" takes you to your game. The password is never typed in the game and, for now, it can't be recovered by email: keep it safe.
- **Closed beta with an invite code:** the first time you open the beta game, you type your invite code and you're in. If the code isn't valid or has already been used, the game tells you and you can type another. The code isn't stored: once you're in, it won't ask again on that device.
- **Clearer account and notices:** account texts no longer name any provider ("Link an account", "Your account"). On the beta server, Options › Account shows "Forget this device" and tells you what happens to your game (if you're a guest without an account, it asks for confirmation because you lose it), the link to change your password and the status of browser sign-in. If the device no longer has the session, you can sign in with your account from the loading screen; if your access is suspended, you see it along with your support ID. Server errors (you're going too fast, sign-in took too long, friend limit, choose your name first…) have their own text, and if your game is newer than the server, the notice says so instead of asking you to update.
- **Polish:**
  - The season medal shows its art in the archive and when the season closes.
  - Vhassa's boss ("The Price of the Pact") now grants its seal, "Seal of the Pact", and its frame, "Pact Crimson", like the bosses of the other five campaigns.
  - In English, the game always uses the same words: "Opponent", "Forfeit" and "pack"; the "Boosters" screen is now called "Packs".
  - Error notices use singular or plural ("Wait 1 more second") and thousands separators.
  - At 1024×768, the challenger's "+2" health is no longer hidden by the opening notice.
- **More careful languages:** in Spanish and English, quantities use singular or plural depending on the number ("1 card", "3 cards"), large numbers have thousands separators and dates follow the format of the chosen language. If you switch languages mid-match, the table, the combat log and the result window are translated on the spot. In English, campaign stage 4 has the same name as Brakka's ability, "Unleashed Fury". Headers no longer get cut off or wrap with long texts.
- **Season 0 ready for the beta:** the beta season brings its free track (a bone frame at level 1, an emerald one at level 3 and the "Forge Ingot" emblem at level 4) and the beta medal when it closes. It starts with the first beta release. The premium track brings six more frames and four more emblems (tongs, key, sword and the level 10 phoenix).
- **Account level:** your level appears in a steel hexagon next to your name in the lobby, on your profile, on your friend card and on your table plate. It goes up by playing PVP matches that grant rewards and by winning campaign stages for the first time, and it never resets; it isn't your PVP rank. In Profile › Level you see the bar, the milestones every 5 levels and how XP is earned. When you level up, the match report celebrates it with a banner; if you level up outside a match, you see it once in the lobby.
- **Who goes first, by draw:** in every PVP match, friendly ones included, the server draws who goes first. The opening notice says it in a single sentence ("The server drew who goes first…"), and whoever goes second is the challenger: they get the Coin and +2 health, max health included, with a "+2" tag that rises over their bar. The "Went first" and "Challenger" marks stay even if you close and reopen the game. In the campaign you still go first, with no coin or extra health. The "Friendly" chip is now confirmed by the server.
- **Rank icons:** each of the 14 ranks has its own shield. The metal shows the tier (iron, bronze, silver, gold and molten), the wings grow with it and the central motif comes from the rank's name. You see it next to your rank in the lobby and on the PVP card, in "Your rank" and "Your position", on every row of the Leaderboard and on your Profile. When a match ranks you up, the report shows the new shield up large. The top rank's shield pulses (except with "Reduce motion").
- **Season at the end of the match:** when a match gives you season XP, the match report shows how much you earned, your pass level before and after, the progress bar and the piece you reached. If you level up off screen, a notice tells you when you return to the lobby. The Profile has a "Season" group with what you earn in the pass, and from your PVP position you go straight to the Pass.
- **Clearer options:** checkboxes are now toggles, the hint bar shows your controller's buttons (also LB/RB to switch tabs) and, when you close a notice, you return to the tab you were on. In Accessibility, the "+12" healing preview fits inside its box.
- **Hero seals on packs:** each hero pack opens with its color in the runes, the halo, the cracks and the shockwave, and with its own seal: Yrenna, spiraling purple threads; Shouny, a crescent moon and slashes; Brakka, an axe gash and sparks; Krunk, a hammer that crushes the book and cracks the table, and lumps of coal; Sable, a hexagonal barrier that bursts into shards; and Vhassa, pact chains and drops of blood. The seals and the light of the burst glow over the table. With reduced motion or "No animation", only the color changes.
- **Pack batches and a new summary:** when opening several packs you'll see "Pack N of M"; common and uncommon cards flip on their own and you flip rare or better ones yourself (or "Reveal all"). The summary marks each card as "NEW" or "Duplicate · you have N", counts cards by rarity, shows how far you are from the guaranteed legendary and lets you convert duplicates to essence or open another pack without leaving. With several packs, one row per pack. All with a controller.
- **3D pack opening:** when you open a hero pack, its grimoire lands on the table with that hero's cover; drag it to the altar or press "Open". The book fills with golden cracks, bursts into light and the cards fall face down in a row, from most common to rarest, with their aura, and flip to show their new face. About 2.4 s with "Ceremony", half that with "Fast"; "Skip", reduced motion and "No animation" work as always.
- **PVP "Play" revamped:** your rank and the coins you've earned today (with their cap and the time it resets) in view, cards for ranked, casual and private room, and a search card with a timer and "Cancel". The private room shows the code up large with "Copy" and "Close room". If you leave PVP while searching, the search continues: you'll see it in the tabs and in a strip in the lobby, and the campaign and challenges wait until you cancel. Everything works with a controller.
- **Friends revamped:** your friends appear on cards with their avatar and frame, grouped by status (in a match, in the campaign, online and offline), with tabs for requests and blocked players. The "⋯" menu brings together remove and block, and everything works with a controller.
- **Clearer friend challenges:** an incoming or sent challenge appears on a card with both portraits and a 60-second countdown; if it's declined, you'll see the notice and can challenge again after a short wait. On the table, a friendly match carries its mark and you can view your opponent's card.
- **Lobby:** your identity block opens your profile and the Friends card shows whether anyone is online.
- **New first sign-in:** the first time you enter, you choose your hero seeing them in full art, with their passive, their abilities and the deck you start with; LB/RB cycle through the six. Then you choose your name to play as a guest (your progress stays on this device; you can create an account later) and you can go back with "Change hero".
- **"Identity" profile:** your avatars and frames in two tabs, grouped (heroes, emblems, boss seals; basic, campaign, bosses), with a lock and veil on the ones you don't have yet and a "New" mark on recently earned ones. On the right, "How others see you": how your avatar looks with its frame in the lobby and at the PVP table. Pick a piece to see its details and "Equip" it (also with a controller). Your name appears with its number, which only your friends see.
- **PVP with tabs:** Play, History and Leaderboard on the same screen (LB/RB with a controller). History shows each duel in two lines, with the opponent, whether it was ranked or friendly, the result and when; if there are no matches or it couldn't load, it says so and lets you retry. The leaderboard shows the table, "Your position" and "Refresh" with its countdown.
- **New Shop:** packs are shown in a 3×2 showcase with each hero's wax seal and how many you have unopened; when you pick one, its card says what it contains, shows the drop rates, lets you choose the batch and buy (press twice to confirm, with the price in view) and open the ones you already have. If you're short on balance, the button says so. "Packs" in the lobby opens the showcase on the pack you have the most of to open, and "View their packs" on a campaign hero's card opens their pack directly.
- **New Collection:** per-hero tabs with their portrait and how many of their cards you have; a bar with search, "All · Owned · Missing", type, cost and "More" (rarity, pack and sort order); a line that tells you what you're viewing, with filters you can remove one by one or all at once. If there's nothing to show, it tells you why and what to do. "Convert duplicates" shows beforehand how much essence you'll get and asks for confirmation with a breakdown by rarity; cards you own below the maximum offer "Get another copy". With a controller: LB/RB switch heroes, X opens "More" and search opens the on-screen keyboard. If you come from a deck, "Back to deck" takes you back to it, and filters are remembered while you play.
- **Inspect and browse the table with a controller:** Y opens and closes the inspection of the focused card (it no longer opens on its own when moving through your hand or a grid, so leaving takes a single B again). On the table, the inspected card is shown above your hand, with an arrow pointing to it, and ◄ ► move to the neighboring card. Clicking or pressing Y on a portrait opens the hero's card: who went first and why, their passive, their abilities and their equipment. Pressing up from your hand takes you into the table: you move through portraits, equipment, stats and resources with the D-pad, and Y shows the help for each. With a mouse, tooltips appear after 0.3 s, in a larger font.
- **Vhassa, the Pactbound:** the new hero pays with her own health. Her Blood Tribute costs her health so her next attack hits harder (wax seals next to your attack show how much), and you can use it several times in the same turn; her Crimson Rite hits harder the more health she's missing. Also arriving: her pack, a 4-fight campaign and the "First Debt" deck for beating the hard fight.
- **My decks:** from "Decks" in the lobby you see the decks of each unlocked hero next to their portrait, with name, "20/20" and a mini cost curve. "+ New deck" appears if that hero has fewer than 3.
- **Forge Table:** the new editor shows your cards with art, gold cost and how many copies you're running. Filter by cost, type or source and search by name without worrying about capitals or accents. The deck panel brings together the hero portrait, the card counter, the cost curve, the breakdown by type and the list grouped by cost.
- **New cards, "Forge Window":** in Collection, packs, deck builder and inspector, each card shows its art in a window, the cost, the name on a plate with the rarity diamond, the type with its icon (with a lock if it's exclusive to a hero), rules of up to 3 or 4 lines and, at the bottom, Power, Armor, Durability or Charges with their icon. The border shows the rarity, and rare, epic and legendary cards have a shine. In Collection, a bottom bar shows your copies ("2/3"), the essence the extras would give or "Missing · Get" if you don't have it; there are no more buy buttons on the card: "Get" opens a window to craft or buy it. In packs, rarity is read on the same bar, and in the campaign the card you choose as a reward is marked with a halo and a ✓.
- **Your hand with the new cards:** in combat, each card in your hand shows full-width art with the cost, the name with the rarity diamond, the type with its icon and, on weapons, armor and trinkets, their stats in view. The full text is read by inspecting the card, which also shows the stats and, for exclusives, which hero they belong to.
- **If startup fails, you'll understand why:** the loading screen says what happened and that your cards and progress are saved, with a bigger "Retry" button. If there's a new version of the game, it tells you and offers "Quit game" instead of a retry that couldn't work.
- **Loading, step by step:** while the game starts you see which step it's on ("Step 5 of 6") and a six-segment bar, one per step, that actually fills up instead of just moving. If something takes longer than usual, a card tells you the game is retrying and which attempt it's on. If it fails, the screen says which step it stopped at. Without an internet connection, it tells you so plainly and tries again only when the network comes back. With "Reduce motion", the bar doesn't animate.
- **From startup to lobby, without jumps:** the loading screen shows a more compact emblem, with the game's name and a gold rule underneath, and when it's done the lobby (or whichever screen is next) appears with a soft fade instead of a cut. The lobby's top bar now has only the game's name, more legible. With "Reduce motion" or "Instant" speed, the change is immediate. The gold rule under the name looks gold on large screens too, and the "Waiting for connection..." notice fits on a single line.
- **Errors, in their place:** when something fails in the Shop, Packs, the Campaign, PVP, your profile or the match, the notice appears inside that screen, with a title that says what kind of problem it is, the reason and, when useful, a button to retry. No more stray technical messages in red. History and the leaderboard show their error separately, with "Retry", without confusing it with "no matches yet".
- **Brief notices and empty lists:** controller connected or disconnected notices appear at the top center, one at a time, with a bar that empties in 3 seconds; if several arrive, you'll see "+1". In a match, if notifications drop, a discreet box tells you the match continues and reconnects on its own, and when it's back you'll see "Connection restored". Friends, History, the leaderboard and the campaign with no content show a clear empty state: what's going on and, if there's something to do, a button ("Copy your name", "Go to PVP", "Ranked match").
- **Abilities with icons:** your two abilities have their icon, their name and their cost. If you don't have enough energy they look dimmed, but you can focus them with the controller and press Y (or the inspect shortcut) to read what they do and how much energy you're missing.
- **Health on the table:** each hero's health sits on a plate like the attack and armor ones, with a "forge" bar of molten metal level with the number, marks every 5 and every 10 and veins that move slowly (still with "Reduce motion"). The opponent's side is mirrored. At 25% health or less, the plate and the bar turn red and pulse (with "Reduce motion", they only change color).
- **Symmetrical combat table:** your energy, hand, deck and discard pile sit on a strip under your portrait, just like the opponent's under theirs, and the opponent's card count and their Challenger's Obol are shown on their strip. When a PVP match starts (and the first time in the campaign) a brief notice says who goes first and who is the challenger; it disappears on its own or with your first action, which still goes through. A "Went first" or "Challenger" mark stays in the corner of each portrait, with its explanation on mouse hover. If you aim at a card you can't afford, your strip shows the crystals you're missing in red. If you're the challenger in PVP, your Challenger's Obol glows for a moment when the match starts so you spot it in your hand.
- **Hand cards:** each card's art is now centered; before, it shifted to the right and got cut off at the edge, especially on small screens. The Coin no longer looks transparent during the opponent's turn.
- **Show missing:** turn on this toggle to see the cards you don't have yet in gray and open them in Collection, where you can craft or buy them.
- **Edit before playing:** in PVP and in the Campaign you have "Edit deck" next to the chosen deck, or "Build a deck" if your hero doesn't have one. When you leave the editor you return to where you were.
- **Friends:** in the lobby, "Friends". Add someone with their full name (for example, "Aria#1234"; yours appears at the top with a button to copy it), accept or decline requests and block anyone you don't want to see. You see whether your friends are online, in a match or in the campaign.
- **Friend challenges:** "Challenge" invites an online friend to a direct match with your PVP deck. The invitation lasts 60 seconds. Challenges are friendly: they give no gold and don't change your rank.
- **Converting duplicates to essence, now you can see it:** the essence comes out of your duplicate cards and flies to the essence icon, which pulses with each fragment that arrives.
- **Profile icons:** tap your portrait in the lobby to choose an avatar and a frame. There are 16 avatars (the heroes you own, emblems and boss seals) and 8 frames. The ones you don't have yet show a lock and tell you how to get them: unlocking heroes, progressing in the campaign and defeating bosses. It's all free.
- **Your opponent sees your icon:** in PVP matches, your avatar and frame appear next to your name, and your opponent's next to theirs.
- **What you earn stays:** the icons you get aren't lost even if progress is reset.

### Changes

- **Texts that fit:** lobby buttons trim a name that doesn't fit with "…" instead of overflowing, and in English the Spanish text no longer flashes for an instant when a screen opens.
- **Effects easier on the eyes:** "Reduce motion and flashes" now also removes the flashes from hits and from turn and energy notices. Pack opening and essence conversion respect the same flash limit as combat. And if you change an effects option while a pack is opening, the animation is skipped without losing any card.
- **Healing has its icon:** the healing number has a cross, so it doesn't rely on color to stand apart from damage.
- **Cleaner pack opening:** the golden cut stays inside the pack instead of crossing the whole frame.
- **Deck changes kept safe:** the editor shows whether it's saved or how many cards are missing. If you try to leave, switch decks or go to Collection with pending changes, you can save and exit with 20 cards, discard the changes or keep editing. Deleting a deck requires a two-step confirmation.
- **Decks with controller, mouse or touch:** with a controller, A adds or removes depending on the area, X removes a copy, Y inspects, LB/RB change the cost, Menu saves and B goes back; focus stays put when adding or removing. With a keyboard, Q/E change the cost and Delete removes. With a mouse, click adds, right-click removes and hovering shows the card; on touch, tap to add or press and hold to inspect.
- **Revamped lobby:** the PVP and PVE cards have a band with your rank, your hero and your deck, or with your next fight and how far you are in the campaign, next to a seal with the hero's portrait or the campaign art. Below, six matching buttons. With a controller, Start opens Options.
- **Hero campaigns and boss exclusive:** each hero campaign shows its four fights (Easy, Normal, Hard and Boss) on the route, with the opponent and what each one awards, and a hero card that says whether you already have them or what it takes to earn them, with "View their packs". In the campaign selector, each hero campaign says whether its hero is already yours. Heroes you've just earned carry a "New" mark until you pick them. After beating the boss, you choose your exclusive card on its own screen: the ones you don't have first, a bar with your choice and "Take this card" in two steps so you don't make a mistake, or "Choose later".
- **Duel report (end of match):** when it's over you see which match it was, against whom, why it ended and on which turn ("Your opponent's health reached 0 · turn 7", "You forfeited on turn 5"). In the campaign, the loot arrives as chips (coins, essence, packs, cards) and the coins and essence fly from the opponent to their chip; if you lose, you see what was at stake. Avatars, frames, heroes and exclusives you earn appear below. In PVP, your rank with the points gained or lost, your streak and today's coins. The buttons change depending on the case: "Next fight" or "Retry" in the campaign, "Find another" in PVP. A press made right as the window opens doesn't count, so you don't skip it by accident. A defeat says "You lost".
- **Two-column campaign window:** on the left you choose hero and deck; on the right you see the whole route, all 10 stages without scrolling, also with a controller at 4:3. Each stage shows its opponent, the first-win reward (coins, essence or a card) and "Play" on the next one; cleared ones say "Cleared". The chosen hero's card, with their passive and abilities, stays in view instead of the pop-up box, and "View hero" (Y on a controller) opens it in full. The deck is chosen from a dropdown that shows how many cards it has; same in PVP. With a controller, LB and RB jump between your hero and the route, and focus starts on the next stage.
- **Three-column campaigns:** when choosing a campaign you always see three columns, also with a controller on 4:3 screens, and all six campaigns fit without scrolling. Each one shows its art, its name, a progress bar and "1/10 cleared"; hero campaigns count their fights, "0/4 fights", same as inside. The one you've started carries the "In progress" mark (with a controller, focus starts there) and completed ones a ✓ over the dimmed art. Your coins and essence are at the top right.
- **Confirming works the same across the game:** Delete deck, Forfeit, Craft, Buy a card, Restore defaults and Quit game ask for a second press the same way: the button turns into a question, a bar empties in 3 seconds and, if you wait or move to another control, it's canceled.
- **Clearer windows:** the shop's drop rates, video settings, account decisions, the on-screen keyboard, the match menu and the log now use the same kind of window.
- **Friends:** "Remove" and "Block" ask for confirmation in their own window, and a challenge card always appears on top of everything else. Friends and Profile show the controller hint bar.
- **Support ID:** in Options, Account, "Copy support ID" copies your identifier so you can send it to the game team if they ask. It isn't shown on screen.
- **Studio logo:** the game now opens with the Wildcat Games logo.
- **Lobby with new art:** the PVP card shows Brakka bringing his axe down on Sable's shield, and the PVE card, Shouny on the battlefield with the citadel behind her.
- **Each pack with its hero:** when you open a Brakka, Sable, Yrenna, Shouny, Krunk or Vhassa pack, the pack that breaks carries that hero's seal: the axe in embers, the shield, the spool and needle, the moon with the dagger, the scrap-metal maul or the wax seal with chains.
- **Hero Duel CCG:** the game debuts its name on the loading screen, in the lobby and in the window. Because of the name change, the first time you open this version your settings (language, volume and display) go back to defaults. If you were playing without linking an account, you start with a new profile; if you linked it to your account, sign in with it to get it back.
- **Ranks with new names:** the ranks of the competitive ladder have new names. Points and rules don't change: you keep your rank, only its name changes.
- **Revamped combat table:** your hero and the opponent face off with large portraits, each with their weapon, armor and trinket alongside, showing the equipped card's art and its durability or charges. Each hero's health is shown large under their portrait. Your abilities and your energy are at the bottom left, next to your hand, and "Attack" and "Pass" at the bottom right.
- **Menu and log at hand:** "Forfeit" is in the menu (top-left button) and the match log opens with the top-right button; a gold dot lets you know when there are new plays. Both close with Esc, with the controller's B (or Circle) button or by clicking outside. With a controller, Start opens the menu and Select opens the log.
- **Clearer hand:** only the cards you can play show "Play", and they rise with a golden halo; the rest are dimmed, without the old "No energy" button. Your hand fans out and, with many cards on 4:3 screens, cards overlap instead of spilling off the table.
- **Turn in view:** the turn badge turns gold when it's your turn and holds the PVP match timer inside; a golden halo surrounds the hero who is playing. The opponent's hand shows as card backs under the badge, with their count and the Coin beside them, and a faint "VS" fills the center of the table. The campaign reward no longer shows "+0 essence".
- **Tabbed options:** options are no longer a side panel: they open in a centered window with six tabs (General, Video, Audio, Accessibility, Controls and Account). Switch tabs with a click, with LB/RB on a controller or with Q/E on a keyboard. The settings are the same and apply instantly; "Quit game" sits at the bottom, next to "Close".
- **Restore defaults:** every Options tab (except Account) has "Restore defaults", which resets only that tab. It asks for confirmation with a second tap. Language isn't touched. "Quit game" also asks for confirmation.
- **Custom damage color:** in Options, Accessibility, next to "Neutral" and "Red" there's a third button, "Custom". Choosing it shows a palette of 8 named colors for damage numbers, chosen so they aren't confused with healing for color-blind players, and a preview shows how it will look. "Restore defaults" sets it back to neutral.
- **Options during a match:** the match menu (top-left button) has "Options", above "Forfeit". They open over the table, without "Quit game" so you don't abandon the match by mistake, and when you close them you return to your hand.
- **Gold energy:** energy crystals are now gold, like card costs. Teal is now reserved for essence.
- **Farewell to the "Penumbral Shadows" set:** its 20 cards move to the main set of Expansion 0. They stay in your collection, in your decks and in the same packs; only the "Penumbra" filter disappears from the Collection. Shouny's pack is still called "The Threads of Penumbra".
- **"Gifts" filter:** in the Collection, the "Gift / Promo" filter is now called "Gifts". It gathers the cards that don't come in any pack, such as Apocalypse, which can still be crafted.

### Fixes

- The victory shine crosses the opponent's name and no longer leaves a smudge over the trinket slot.
- In the Collection, cards you don't have yet no longer look empty or cut off while scrolling the list.
- Match notices (for example, "You already used that ability this turn.") no longer linger in the lobby when you go back.
- With a controller or keyboard, you can now reach the hero's abilities in combat with the D-pad or arrow keys.
