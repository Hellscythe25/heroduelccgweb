### Fixes

- **Hand full of cards**: with 9 or more cards in hand, the extra ones no longer went off screen (with no way to play them). Now the cards shrink to fit in a single row and you can always play any of them.
- **Stale counter in the Shop**: the Shop no longer shows an "unopened packs" message that was left over from a previous purchase and didn't match what you actually had.
- **Duplicate decks**: saving a deck by clicking Save several times in a row no longer creates repeated copies of the same deck.
- **Purchases with the exact balance**: buying packs with the exact balance (or double-clicking the buy button) can no longer produce a confusing error — the Shop now always responds with a clear message.
- **Deleting decks**: confirming a deck deletion with a quick double click no longer shows the "That deck doesn't exist" error — the deck is deleted only once, no surprises.
