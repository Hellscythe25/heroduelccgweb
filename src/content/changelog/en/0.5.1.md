### New

- **Your hero's signature, with real particles**: when you play a card, Brakka releases embers with rising golden sparks; Sable, a ring of steel glints; Yrenna, a spiral of arcane motes; Shouny, floating wisps of moonlight; and Krunk, falling dust and shards.
- **Opening a pack is a small ritual**: the pack appears with a golden halo, shakes and breaks into fragments before the cards come out. It lasts just over a second, you can skip it like the rest of the reveal and, if you open several packs at once, it plays only once.
- **Each rarity shines its own way when revealed**: Rare cards, with a ring of blue glints; Epic cards, with a violet burst; and Legendary cards, with a golden burst. The Divine Pack also brings a shower of golden and violet glints.
- With “Reduce motion and flashes” there are no particles and the pack simply appears and fades out. With “No animation” there is no ritual.
