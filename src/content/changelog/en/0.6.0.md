### New

- **Music and sound effects**: the game now has sound. There is music for the menu, combat, pack opening, victory and defeat, and effects for hits, gear, cards, packs and buttons. In Options you have Master, Music, Effects and Interface volume, and “Mute when the game is in the background” (the your-turn and match-found alerts still play).
- **Clearer combat**: a big notice when your turn starts; your health shown large and the rest of the stats (energy, attack, armor and deck) as an icon and a number, in the same order for you and for your opponent; a log that is easy to read; an end-of-match screen with the result, and forfeiting with confirmation.
- **Packs**: cards look bigger, rarity is clearly readable, cards that are new to your collection are marked and at the end you choose “Continue” or “Open another.”
- **Collection and shop**: cards you are missing are shown in gray and duplicates show how many copies you have. Buttons you can't use tell you why, and pack drop rates are shown in a table.
- **PC**: video options for display mode, resolution, vertical sync and FPS limit. If you don't like a change, it reverts on its own after 10 seconds.
- **Controller**: the selected element is always visible, there are button prompts for your controller (Xbox, PlayStation or generic) and an on-screen keyboard to type names and codes. The controller vibrates on heavy hits and on legendaries (you can turn it off in Options, “Controller vibration”), and we let you know if it disconnects.
- **Touch screens**: buttons are bigger and you check a card's information by pressing and holding it.

### Fixes

- Pack fragments no longer vanish all at once when the first rare card shines.
- In 4:3 and widescreen windows, packs and combat are shown in full.

### Text changes

- In Spanish, «Craftear» is renamed «Fabricar».
