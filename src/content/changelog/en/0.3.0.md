### New

- **Batch packs**: opening several packs (up to 10) is now much faster — they all open in a single operation and are revealed one by one, as always.
- **Player names**: in a match you can see who you are playing against ("Hero — Player"), and History saves your opponent's name for every new match.
- **Refresh button in Leaderboard**: refresh the table whenever you want (with a short 30-second wait between refreshes).
- **Type filter in Decks**: filter your collection by Instant, Weapon, Armor or Trinket while you build your deck.
- **Confirmation when deleting decks**: deleting a deck now asks for a second confirmation click — no more accidental deletions.
- **"Quit game" button** in the main menu.
- **Visible version**: the loading screen and the menu show the game version, so it's easy to know which one you have installed.
- **Player name in the menu**: the menu header shows "Player: <your name>".

### Card balance

- **Surge** (neutral, common): cost 2 → **1**. It cost 2 energy and gave 2 energy — it did nothing. Now it actually generates energy.
- **War Cry** (neutral, rare): cost 3 → **2**. It was strictly worse than Brakka's version for the same cost.
- **Arcane Storm** (Yrenna, rare): cost 5 → **4**. It was too expensive for what it does.

### Fixes

- Removed the confusing error messages in the middle of a match (like "It's not your turn." when you had in fact been able to click): they were races between your action and your opponent's, and now the screen simply updates itself with the real state.
- **Spanish texts reviewed**: the whole game now uses neutral Spanish.
- Text fixes and general stability during duels.
